The comments, JSDoc, README, and examples no longer name the client sites Astroid's patterns came from. Each one keeps the reason it gave and describes the site generically instead, and the config examples use Google's example conventions: `key: "example"`, "Example Organization", and `example.com`. The JSDoc ships in the `.d.ts` files, so your editor's hover text changes. No code, default, or behavior changed, and there is nothing to do when you upgrade.
